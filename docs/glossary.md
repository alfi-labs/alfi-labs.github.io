# Glossary of Meta-Scientific Astrology [MSA]

## Core Theoretical Concepts

### [The Field]
**Definition**: The single, dynamic, informational foundation of all existence.
**Context**: In MSA, [The Field] is not a specific physical field (like electromagnetic) but a philosophical category representing the unity and process-based nature of reality (being-as-becoming). It is the medium in which all "things" exist as stable patterns.

### [Proto-Reality]
**Definition**: The level of pure potential of [The Field].
**Context**: A state where all outcomes exist as information in a probabilistic state (quantum superposition). This layer of reality is fluid, multivariant, and illogical (by classical standards). It is directly experienced by human consciousness during dreams and Altered States of Consciousness (ASCs).

### [Consensus-Reality]
**Definition**: The single, actual result of the continuous "collapse" of potential.
**Context**: The manifested world that we all share and observe. It is the "frozen" state of [The Field] where probabilities have collapsed into a single, stable outcome. Corresponds to the waking state of consciousness.

### [Body-Spacesuit]
**Definition**: The physical body viewed as a specialized interface.
**Context**: A biological "device" that enables non-local consciousness to interact with the material world. It acts as a filter, tuned to specific frequencies of [The Field] based on its genetics and moment of activation (birth).

### [Synchronism]
**Definition**: The principle of universal alignment with [The Field]'s rhythms.
**Context**: The idea that all systems in the Universe are instinctively synchronized with the fundamental rhythms of [The Field], much like musicians following a conductor. This explains why planetary cycles correlate with earthly events without requiring direct physical causation.

### [Resonant Imprint]
**Definition**: The unique configuration of a system's sensitivity.
**Context**: A "snapshot" of [The Field]'s rhythms fixed at the moment of a system's activation (e.g., birth). It determines the system's "resonant profile"—which frequencies it is most sensitive to throughout its existence. In astrology, this is represented by the **Natal Chart**.

### [Resonance]
**Definition**: The dynamic activation of internal potential.
**Context**: The event that occurs when a current pattern in [The Field] (Transit) matches a pattern in the system's Resonant Imprint. It is the mechanism by which objective time cycles manifest as subjective experiences or events.

## Applied Concepts

### Geocentric Perspective
**Definition**: The view from Earth.
**Context**: Describes the **Map of Perception**—how an observer experiences reality subjectively. It correlates with psychological processes and the "human" experience of events.

### Heliocentric Perspective
**Definition**: The view from the Sun.
**Context**: Describes the **Field of Potentials**—the objective, non-personalized state of the Solar System. It represents the "weather" of [The Field] independent of any observer.

### Vector
**Definition**: A specific combination of planetary strategies.
**Context**: In MSA analysis, a Vector describes a synthesized behavioral strategy derived from the interaction of key planets (e.g., Sun, Moon, MC).
